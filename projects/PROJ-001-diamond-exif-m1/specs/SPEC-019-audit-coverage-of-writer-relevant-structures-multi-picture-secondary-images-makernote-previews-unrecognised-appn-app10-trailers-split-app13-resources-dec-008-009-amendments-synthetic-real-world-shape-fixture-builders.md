# SPEC-019: Audit coverage of writer-relevant structures: Multi-Picture secondary images, MakerNote previews, unrecognised APPn (APP10), trailers, split APP13 resources; DEC-008/009 amendments; synthetic real-world-shape fixture builders

> **OUTLINE — `cycle: frame`.** This spec exists so its ID is stable and
> siblings can declare `depends_on: [SPEC-019]`. Capture **scope** (Context /
> Goal / Non-Goals) and **dependencies** only — the *approach* is designed
> just-in-time when this moves to `design`. Do not pre-design it here.

## Context

SPIKE-001 (archived, second pass included) found structures in real files that the audit does not report and a strip must handle: Multi-Picture secondary images (4 of 11 JPEGs; the Nikon extras carry their own Exif, the iPhone extras XMP and ICC), preview JPEGs inside MakerNotes, proprietary APP10 `AROT` segments, a non-Exif trailer after EOI, and Photoshop image resources split across two APP13 segments (0 IPTC regions found where ExifTool lists 2). Until the audit sees them, DEC-009's preview can say a policy leaves nothing when it does not. Real files cannot be committed, so this spec also builds synthetic fixtures for each shape (a Multi-Picture JPEG, a split-APP13 JPEG, an absolute-offset MakerNote, a 64 KB-limit Exif, a trailer, an APP10).

## Goal

The audit reports Multi-Picture images, MakerNote-embedded previews, unrecognised APPn segments, trailers and joined split APP13 resources as inventory items and findings (new DEC-008 categories or region kinds, with DEC-009 cells and may-hold rows), each shown against ExifTool where ExifTool lists them, with synthetic builders for every shape and the report renderers and presets updated.

## Non-Goals

- Any writing or stripping (the next specs)
- Parsing MakerNotes or decoding the embedded images' contents beyond locating them and the metadata groups they hold
- PNG, WebP, HEIC
